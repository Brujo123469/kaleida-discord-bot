# Kaleida server rules

1. **Be decent.** Disagree, critique the game, argue about builds - but no insults, harassment or personal attacks.
2. **No hate.** No slurs, and no attacks on anyone for who they are (race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, nationality and the like).
3. **Keep it safe for work.** No sexual, gory-real-world or shock content. Dark fantasy talk about the game is fine.
4. **No spam, scams or self-promotion.** No unsolicited ads, invite links, "free Nitro", crypto or giveaway posts, and no flooding. Share your own creative work in the channels made for it.
5. **No threats.** Not even as a joke.
6. **Respect privacy.** Never post anyone's personal information.
7. **Stay roughly on topic** for each channel.
8. **No leaks or piracy** of Kaleida or any other game.

**Moderation:** an automated moderator (Spirekeeper, the server bot) reads messages from new members and anything a member flags with 🚩. It can remove clear rule-breaking posts and give short timeouts; everything else goes to a human. Bans are only ever decided by a person. If you think the bot got something wrong, message a moderator.
