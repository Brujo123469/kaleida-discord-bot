# How Kaleida uses AI

I'm an indie developer with a limited budget and a big dream: an MMO with deep, complex systems and real longevity. No pay-to-win, no dailyscape, a healthy early, mid and late game, a good community, good graphics and gameplay, and a game that holds up against bugs and bots. For one person, that's a pipe dream without the tools that keep becoming available. So I use any tool that is ethical and effective, and AI is one of them.

This game doesn't get built with only AI. It needs artists, people who share the vision, and players who know what a game like this needs. That's something AI can never replace: taste. I use AI to augment my vision, like any other tool. If the demo does well and I can secure funding, I hope to bring people on board who will bring their own taste to these same tools.

## What AI does on Kaleida

- **Code and planning.** An AI coding assistant (Claude) writes code, editor scripts and tools with me, and helps me work through designs. I decide what gets built, and I play and test everything it makes.
- **Work inside the engine.** It runs Unreal Engine for me through scripts: placing things, wiring systems together, fitting bought animations onto the game's skeleton. Work I could do by hand, just faster.
- **Animation.** I animate in Cascadeur, which has AI-assisted posing. Some rough base motions start from a text-to-motion model and are then reworked by hand.
- **Placeholder models.** A few placeholder models, like the wagons, started from an image-to-3D tool working from my own concept art. Placeholders are meant to be replaced or reworked.
- **This server.** Spirekeeper uses Claude to give messages a first read and to answer questions from the FAQ. People make the real calls, and bans are only ever decided by a person.

## The lines I hold

- **Nothing here trains AI.** No one's work in this project, mine or anyone else's, is used to train AI models.
- **Bought assets marked "No AI" are never fed into generative AI.** AI may only operate the engine on them, the way any script would. When I'm unsure, I ask the creator: two have confirmed in writing that the way I use their work is fine. Where something crossed my own line, I removed or replaced it.
- **Artists' work is protected both ways.** Work I commission is never fed into a generative tool, and I'll ask artists to do the same for anyone else's work.
